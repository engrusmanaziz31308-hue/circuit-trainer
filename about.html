About Circuit Trainer

Circuit Trainer is a free web app for learning electrical circuits. It brings together a live circuit designer, theory with animated diagrams, calculators, formula sheets and quizzes in one place. It is made for students of electrical technology, electricians, and homeowners who want to understand their wiring better.

What is inside

  • Circuit designer: place parts, wire them and see the circuit respond.
  • Theory: animated explanations of Ohm's law, series and parallel circuits, AC and DC, and more.
  • Calculators: cable size and MCB, voltage drop, power, energy and bills, battery backup, solar and more.
  • Formulas and tables: a quick reference, with CSV and PDF download for the cable tables.
  • Quizzes: hundreds of questions to test yourself.

Our principles

The app is free to use, and we do not collect personal data. Details are in our privacy policy. Safety information is for general awareness and does not replace professional training or advice from a licensed electrician.
