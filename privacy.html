Circuit Trainer ("the app") is an educational tool for learning electrical circuits, calculators, formulas, and quizzes. This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Information we do not collect

The app does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, phone number, email address, or location.

2. Information stored on your device

The app may save your circuit designs, preferences (such as language and toolbar layout), and quiz progress in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

3. Advertising and cookies

If advertising is shown on this site, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads.

4. Children

The app is for general education and is not directed at children under 13.
