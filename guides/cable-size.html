How to Choose Cable Size

Why the right cable matters

The correct cable size depends on three things: the load, the length of the run, and how the cable is installed. An undersized cable overheats, causes voltage drop, and reduces the performance of appliances.

Reading cable sizes in Pakistan

Local cable sizes are usually written as number of strands / diameter of one strand in inches. For example, 3/.029 means 3 strands, each 0.029 inch in diameter. The effective copper area is approximately 1.28 mm², not the nominal 1.5 mm², so the actual cross-section can be smaller than the name suggests. When in doubt, choose the larger size.

Common sizes

Size (mm²)Typical use
1.5Lighting and fan circuits
2.5Sockets and general appliances
4 to 6Air conditioners, geysers, larger motors
10 and aboveMain supply and high loads

Three questions to ask

1. How much load is on the circuit? Add up the power of every appliance, then calculate the current.

2. How far is the run? A longer cable loses more voltage. See the guide on voltage drop.

3. How is the cable installed? A cable inside a conduit in a wall carries less current safely than a cable in open air, because heat escapes less easily.

If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one. The extra copper costs a little more, but it reduces heat, voltage loss and risk.
