Electric Shock: Prevention and First Aid

Essential information for every home

An electric shock can be fatal within seconds. The most important rule is to switch off the power before touching anyone who is in contact with electricity. This guide gives basic awareness only and is not a substitute for proper training.

Immediate steps

  1. Protect yourself. Do not touch the person with bare hands. Switch off the main switch or MCB first.
  2. If the power cannot be cut, move the wire or the person away using dry wood, plastic or rubber. Stay on dry ground.
  3. Call for help. Call 1122 (Rescue) or your local emergency number.
  4. Check breathing and pulse for about 10 seconds. If the person is not breathing normally, start CPR.
  5. If the person is breathing, place them in the recovery position (on their side) and keep watching them.
  6. For burns, cool the area under running water for about 20 minutes, then cover it with a clean cloth.
Formal CPR training is strongly recommended. Contact your local rescue service or health organisation to find a course.

What not to do

  • Do not touch electrical equipment with wet hands or while standing on a wet floor.
  • Do not pull the person away by the wire.
  • Do not put anything on a burn, and do not pull clothing that is stuck to the skin.

Safety at home

Give every circuit its own MCB, have RCCB or RCBO protection installed in wet areas, repair damaged wires and sockets immediately, and keep sockets out of children's reach.
