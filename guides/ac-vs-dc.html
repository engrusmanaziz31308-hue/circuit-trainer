AC vs DC: What Is the Difference?

The basics, explained simply

Direct Current (DC) flows in one direction, as from a battery. Alternating Current (AC) reverses direction many times per second, as in household supply. In Pakistan, the household supply is 230 V AC at 50 Hz.

Voltage and current in AC

AC voltage is always changing, so it is measured as an RMS (effective) value. A 230 V AC supply has the same heating effect as 230 V DC. For resistive loads such as bulbs, heaters and irons, the AC and DC current at the same voltage are equal.

The power difference

In DC, power is simply: Power = Voltage × Current. In AC, there is an extra factor, the power factor: Power = Voltage × Current × Power factor. Motors and fans usually have a power factor below 1.
