How to Calculate Your Electricity Bill

Units and kilowatt-hours made simple

Electricity is billed in units, where one unit equals one kilowatt-hour (kWh). One unit means a 1 kW appliance running for one hour. The more power and the longer it runs, the higher the cost.

The basic formula

Units (kWh) = Watts × Hours per day × Days ÷ 1000

One appliance

A 60 W bulb running 5 hours a day for 30 days:

60 × 5 × 30 ÷ 1000 = 9 units. At Rs 40 per unit, the cost is Rs 360.

Whole home

List every appliance with its power and daily hours, calculate the units for each, and add them up. Air conditioners and geysers usually make the biggest difference, so check them separately.

These are example values. Check the rating label or manual of your own appliances for accurate figures.

Simple savings: use LED lighting, choose energy-efficient appliances (look for star ratings), and switch off what you are not using.
